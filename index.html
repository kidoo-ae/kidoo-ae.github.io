About KIDOO

KIDOO ADVERTISING L.L.C is a Dubai-based print-on-demand studio. We help people and businesses turn ideas into real products — from a single personalised gift to branded merch for a team or event, and on-demand fulfilment for online sellers.

FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can order just one piece.

How do I get a price?

Send us your design and quantity on WhatsApp and we'll reply with a quote.

What files can I send?

Photos, logos or sketches all work. We'll check the quality before printing.

Do you deliver?

Yes, contact us on WhatsApp for delivery options in your area.

Can you dropship orders from my online store?

Yes. We print each order on demand, pack it and deliver it to your customer in the UAE. Message us to set it up.

Can I see a preview first?

Yes. We send a mockup for your approval before we print.
